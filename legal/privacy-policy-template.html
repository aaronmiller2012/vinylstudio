What the app processes

VinylStudio lets you import photographs and create cut-ready designs. The app processes and stores selected photographs, edited artwork, project archives, color palettes, settings, and local Java crash diagnostics on your device. These files can include personal images you choose to open, including photos another app shares with VinylStudio through the Android share sheet or an "Open with" action.

Collection, sharing and network use

The current Android application does not request Internet access, contain advertising or analytics SDKs, or transmit your files to a developer server. Files only leave the app when you explicitly export, back up, or share them using your selected destination, which may be another app or cloud provider with its own policies. When you choose "Share sheets" after an export, VinylStudio grants only the app you pick temporary read access to copies of those SVG/DXF cut files; the copies are deleted from VinylStudio's cache after 24 hours. Please review privacy practices of your chosen destination.

Crash reports

If VinylStudio closes unexpectedly, it saves one short crash report on your device containing the error, the app build, the Android version and the phone model. It contains no photos, project files or file names. The next time you open the app, you may review the report and choose to share it with an app of your choice (for example, email); nothing is sent automatically, and declining has no effect on the app.

Storage, retention and deletion

Work in progress and saved projects are stored locally in application-private storage, and manually exported files are stored wherever you select. Project files remain until you delete them through the app or erase its data. Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently erase projects that you have not backed up. Files exported to other providers must be deleted with those providers.

Security

The application uses Android application-private storage for working files. Android cloud backup is disabled, so VinylStudio data is never uploaded to a backup service. On Android 12 and newer, when you set up a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer (cable or local Wi-Fi), your saved project library, saved color palettes and material calibration records are copied directly to the new phone; editor drafts, caches and crash reports are not. You control any exported backups and the security of external destinations.
